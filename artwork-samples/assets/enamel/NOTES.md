# Enamel specialty-track badges (79 assets)
Generated by `artwork-samples/enamel/` (shield.js shared body + g1..g5 per-track generators), emitted with `retrofit-enamel.js`.
- All sizes are `estimated` (1.0-1.5 in wide by class); not from a regulation. Replace with supplier/regulation values when known.
- Gates: 79/79 pass hard gates. Lowest silhouette fits: emt-intermediate 0.80, emt-paramedic 0.82, buddist-chaplin 0.84, muslim-chaplin 0.84.
- Levels: technician = shield only, senior = star on post, master = wreathed star. Emblem drawn once per track.
- Known differences: engraved texture/gloss not reproduced (flat enamel), stars/wreaths are clean facets, eagles (Comms, IG, Cadet, council, NRA) are stylised.
- Generators keep parameters in named tables at the top of each make-*.js.
