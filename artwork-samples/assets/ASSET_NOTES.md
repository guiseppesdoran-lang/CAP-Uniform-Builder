## Estimated, and why
- Device sizes: DoD/AF attachments are commonly 3/16 in (stars) and 1/4 in (V); applied here from general knowledge, not from a CAP document.
- Triangle and numeral sizes: no source at all; picked so a device sits inside a ribbon bar at plausible proportion.
- Ground team badge: only the reference raster (240 x 203 px, unknown scale) exists.
- 13 ribbons keep `color_verified_against_regulation: false` (colors came from repo art, not CAPR 39-3).

## Conventions in every file
- root width/height in inches; viewBox = 0 0 (w*100) (h*100)  (1 unit = 0.01 in)
- `<metadata>` JSON: size, size_source, citation/basis, anchors, dimensions
- anchors (always): top_center, bottom_center, center, left_edge, right_edge; plus features (star_center, apex, device_N_center, ...)
- ids prefixed `<asset-id>__`; no `<text>`, `<filter>`, `data:` URIs
- `<id>.spec.json` beside each svg (same metadata + fit)
- digits and "GT" are Arial outlines converted to paths by `tools/glyphpath.swift` (macOS CoreText)
