Uniform Builder style guide

How the app looks, why, and the rules for changing it. The values below come straight from styles/app.css: change a token there and this page updates with the app.

This is an unofficial tool. It follows the public CAP Brand Guide for color and type only. It uses no CAP logo, seal, wordmark or emblem, and it must keep saying it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Civil Air Patrol or the U.S. Air Force.

Premise and source boundary

The look is the Civil Air Patrol Brand Guide's own palette and typefaces (Colors and Typography pages of the CAP Brand Portal), applied to a working tool. Nothing else is borrowed: no templates, UI kits or third-party icon sets. The goal is a calm, legible interface for cadets and staff on a phone or a desktop, where the uniform preview is the focus and the controls stay out of its way.

Out of bounds: the CAP logo, symbol, wordmark, command emblem, official seal and legacy graphics; the limited-use colors CAP Navy #111728 and Sky Blue #99B9CA (they need approval from CAP/MC); any wording or layout that makes the app look like an official CAP product.

Shape and space

  • Radius: 6px for controls (--r), 8px for containers (--r-lg). Nothing else.
  • Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px (--s1 to --s6).
  • Targets: at least 44px tall for anything tapped or clicked.
  • Elevation: one shadow (--shadow), used for the command bar, toast and dialogs. Cards use a border, not a shadow.
  • Layout: a 380px sidebar and the preview. Below 768px the sidebar is a drawer and the command bar wraps.

Behavior

  • Focus: every interactive element shows a 3px ring on :focus-visible. Never remove it.
  • Motion: 120 to 200ms state changes only. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Messages: short messages use the toast (capubNotify()); errors stay longer. Do not use alert().
  • Dialogs: Escape closes, Tab stays inside, focus returns to the opener.
  • Undo: setup changes can be undone and redone (Cmd or Ctrl+Z), up to 50 steps.
  • Theme: follows the system; the header button overrides it and the choice is kept in this browser.
  • Preview: the stage is white paper in both themes, and its geometry belongs to the placement harnesses. Do not resize or move it with styling.

Invariants and reopening

  • Keep: brand palette and the two typefaces; 44px targets; visible focus; one primary action per area; the unofficial notice in the header tag, footer and About.
  • May vary: layout of new panels, copy, new status states built from the existing tokens.
  • Needs a decision first: any new color, any new font, anything that draws a CAP mark, changes to the preview stage.

Checking a change

  • npm test, npm run check:assets (run npm run stamp:assets after editing a script or the stylesheet).
  • Both harnesses in scripts/: compareToBaseline() and comparePlacement() report clean: true.
  • Look at it at 375px, 768px and 1280px, in light and dark, and tab through it.
