        About this tool

        An unofficial planning tool for arranging Civil Air Patrol uniform items. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Civil Air Patrol or the U.S. Air Force, and it does not replace CAPR 39-1 or your unit's guidance. Check the regulation before you buy or wear anything.

        Privacy. Usage counts are collected only to improve this tool. Your setup is saved in this browser as you work, so you can pick it up later. A member report you import is read on your device only. The site counts anonymous usage (which uniforms are built and for which grade, how many items are on them, which buttons are used) and gets the feedback you choose to send. A random number kept in this browser tells a returning visit from a first one; it is not linked to you. No name, CAPID, nameplate text or report contents ever leave your browser, member report searches are never stored, and with Do Not Track on only plain visit and build counts are sent and no number is kept.

        Civil Air Patrol, the U.S. Air Force and other names, insignia and uniform designs shown here belong to their owners. The colors and typefaces follow the public CAP Brand Guide. That does not make this an official CAP product.
