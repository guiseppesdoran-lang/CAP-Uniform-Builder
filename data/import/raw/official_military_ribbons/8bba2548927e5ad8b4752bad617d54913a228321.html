Coast Guard Basic Training Honor Graduate Ribbon AP4.1.2.81.1. Established by the Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard, March 3, 1984.

Effective April 1, 1984, U.S. Coast Guard Service members in the top 3 percent of each U.S. Coast Guard recruit training graduating class are eligible for that award. Prior service members who graduated from other than U.S. Coast Guard recruit training are not eligible.
